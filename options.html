Watching

Choose which film(s) to get notified about from the main popup - click the extension icon, pick a movie, then tap "Get notified for all screenings". You can do this for several films at once.

Auto-check
Checks by itself every few minutes, catching slots even while you're not looking. Turn off to only check when you tap "Check now" yourself.

When a followed film opens up, a browser window opens straight to that screening - finish checkout there yourself. Toggles and the interval apply instantly - no need to hit Save.

Alerts

Desktop notification
A pop-up notification and a red badge on the toolbar icon when a slot opens.

Chrome can't actually tell us whether your operating system is blocking notifications - the only way to know for sure is to send one and check.
